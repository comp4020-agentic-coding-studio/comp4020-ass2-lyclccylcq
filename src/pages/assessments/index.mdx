## ANU grading scale

All three tasks use the [official ANU standard grading
scale](https://www.anu.edu.au/students/program-administration/assessments-exams/grading-scale).
The teaching team applies these grade bands to the marking approach stated in
each brief.

| Grade | Mark | Standard |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| HD — High Distinction | 80–100 | Exceptional quality |
| D — Distinction | 70–79 | Superior quality |
| CR — Credit | 60–69 | Good quality |
| P — Pass | 50–59 | Satisfactory quality |
| N — Fail | 0–49 | Learning outcomes have not been demonstrated |
