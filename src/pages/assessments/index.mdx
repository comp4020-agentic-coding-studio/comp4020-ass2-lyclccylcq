The course grade combines **three assignments worth 80%** with **tutorial
participation worth 20%**. Together, the components below account for 100% of
the course mark.

<AssessmentsGrid />

## Tutorial participation — 20%

There are ten marked tutorials, running consecutively from **Week 2 to Week
11**. Each tutorial is worth 2 marks, or 2 percentage points of the final
course mark.

| Component in each tutorial | Mark per tutorial | Total across 10 tutorials |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Attendance and participation | 1 | 10 |
| Photograph submitted during the tutorial | 1 | 10 |
| **Total** | **2** | **20** |

The participation mark is awarded for attending and taking part in the
tutorial activities. The photograph mark requires the tutorial photograph to
be submitted during that tutorial.

## ANU grading scale

All three tasks use the [official ANU standard grading
scale](https://www.anu.edu.au/students/program-administration/assessments-exams/grading-scale).
The teaching team applies these grade bands to the marking approach stated in
each brief.

| Grade | Mark | Standard |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| HD — High Distinction | 80–100 | Exceptional quality |
| D — Distinction | 70–79 | Superior quality |
| CR — Credit | 60–69 | Good quality |
| P — Pass | 50–59 | Satisfactory quality |
| N — Fail | 0–49 | Learning outcomes have not been demonstrated |
