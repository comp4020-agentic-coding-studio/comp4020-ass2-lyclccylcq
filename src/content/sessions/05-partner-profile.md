## Before the session

Ask your partner to bring three photographs of themselves they like and one
they do not. They should choose the images without coaching from you. Bring
your Week 4 notes as a starting point, not a conclusion.

## In the session

Interview each other in pairs. Ask what the subject notices first, which
features or angles they care about, whether they prefer posed or observed
moments, and where the finished photograph will be used.

Turn those answers into a one-page profile. Separate firm boundaries from
preferences worth testing. Include visual references, but describe what works
in each reference rather than asking to reproduce it exactly.

## Afterwards

Share the profile with the person it describes and let them revise it. Submit
only the agreed version. You will use it to plan Assessment 1 and revisit it
after the final shoot.
