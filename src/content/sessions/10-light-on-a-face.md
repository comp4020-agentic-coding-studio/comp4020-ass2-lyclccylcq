## Before the session

Scout one area containing direct light, open shade, a window or doorway and a
possible backlit position. Bring a white or pale surface that can act as a
simple reflector.

## In the session

Rotate through four available-light stations. At each station, expose for the
face and note the direction, hardness and colour of the light. Keep the framing
close enough that the comparison remains about light rather than location.

Select one station and refine it by changing the subject's orientation before
moving the camera. Watch the eyes, shadow edge and background brightness.

## Afterwards

Submit four unedited comparison frames plus one refined portrait. State why the
chosen light suits this person and where the background still competes with
them.
