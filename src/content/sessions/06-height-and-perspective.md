## Before the session

Bring a camera or phone with grid lines enabled and enough space to step back.
Wear or bring clothing that makes leg and torso proportions easy to compare.

## In the session

Work in pairs and keep the subject in one marked position. Photograph from
chest, waist and knee height, first nearby and then from farther away with a
tighter field of view. Keep the framing as consistent as possible.

Compare the contact sheets at the same size. Look for changes in leg length,
head size, torso compression and the balance between subject and environment.
The subject chooses the most convincing frame before the photographer explains
their own choice.

## Afterwards

Annotate six frames with camera height, distance and the visible result. End
with one rule you can use and one situation where that rule should be broken.
