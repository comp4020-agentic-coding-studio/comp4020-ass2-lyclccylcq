## Before the session

Bring one small everyday object your partner can hold, adjust or interact with.
Choose something that belongs to them rather than a prop selected only for its
appearance.

## In the session

Begin with a deliberately static pose and notice where tension appears. Then
replace instructions about body parts with actions: shift weight while turning,
adjust a sleeve, sit and lean toward a sound, or walk to a clear destination.

After each sequence, the subject rates the prompt for comfort and clarity.
Photographers compare how weight, shoulders and hands changed without being
positioned one joint at a time.

## Afterwards

Submit a six-frame contact sheet covering standing, sitting and movement.
Caption each pair with the prompt used and the subject's response to it.
