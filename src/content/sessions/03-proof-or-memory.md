## Before the session

Bring the Week 2 field plan and choose one ordinary campus location. Avoid the
most obviously picturesque place. The exercise works better when composition,
not spectacle, has to carry the photograph.

## In the session

Make three versions of the same memory. First, photograph the default tourist
frame. Second, change the subject's scale and relationship to the background.
Third, photograph an action that belongs to the place rather than asking the
subject to stand in front of it.

Pin the three frames side by side. Partners identify which image merely proves
presence and which one suggests how the place felt. Discuss the visual evidence
behind that judgement.

## Afterwards

Choose one frame and add a fifty-word rationale explaining the role of the
background, the subject's scale and the moment you selected.
