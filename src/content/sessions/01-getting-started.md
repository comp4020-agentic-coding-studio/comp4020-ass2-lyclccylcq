## Before the session

Bring three photographs of people: one you like, one the photographed person
likes, and one where those opinions differ. Phone images are welcome. Ask
permission before showing any photograph to the group.

## In the session

We begin with four anonymous cases: the tourist photo, the low-angle disaster,
the failed candid and the technically polished portrait its subject dislikes.
In small groups, label the **Image**, **Person**, **Moment** and
**Relationship** systems as successful, questionable or unsuccessful.

Next, apply the same diagnosis to one photograph from each group. The aim is
not to agree on a universal verdict. The useful work is explaining what the
photographer optimised and what they failed to notice.

## Afterwards

Write a short caption for one diagnosed photograph: what went wrong, which
system failed first, and the one decision that could have changed the result.
Keep this diagnosis for comparison with your Week 12 work.
