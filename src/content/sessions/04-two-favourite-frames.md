## Before the session

Bring a sequence of eight to twelve portraits from one short shoot. Do not
pre-select only your strongest images. The exercise needs near-misses and
small differences in expression.

## In the session

The photographer and subject separately choose a first and second favourite.
Neither person sees the other's selection. Each writes down what decided the
choice: expression, proportion, hair, light, gesture, background or the feeling
of recognition.

Reveal both shortlists together. Where they differ, point to the exact visual
detail each person valued. The goal is not to persuade the subject to adopt the
photographer's criteria. It is to learn how their criteria differ.

## Afterwards

Record one preference you discovered and one assumption you need to stop
making. These become the first entries in the Partner Photography Profile.
