## Before the session

Write five prompts without using “pose”, “smile”, “relax” or “act natural”. At
least two prompts must involve movement and one must begin with a question.

## In the session

Test the prompts in groups of three: photographer, subject and observer. The
observer records what changed in the subject's expression and body language,
and whether the photographer allowed enough time for a moment to develop.

Repeat the strongest prompt while changing only the photographer's timing.
Compare a single anticipated frame with a short burst. Select for expression,
not merely sharpness.

## Afterwards

Revise three prompts using the subject's feedback. Keep the exact words and add
one note about when each prompt would feel inappropriate or overused.
