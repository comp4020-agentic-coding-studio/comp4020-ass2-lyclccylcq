Neko Azusa is a professional model, Lion Yuu's regular creative collaborator and
the course's co-convenor. As co-lecturer, Neko Azusa brings the perspective of
the person in front of the camera into every part of the course.

Neko Azusa's teaching focuses on how direction feels from the model's side, why
a technically accomplished portrait can still feel wrong, and how
photographers can build images with — rather than simply of — the person they
photograph.
