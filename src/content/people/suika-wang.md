Suika Wang is a collaborating photographer and tutor for Partner Photography.
Suika supports the practical side of the course, helping students turn an
idea for a portrait into a clear and workable photographic approach.

Through studio activities and feedback, Suika helps students make purposeful
choices about light, framing and timing while keeping the photographed person
at the centre of the process.
