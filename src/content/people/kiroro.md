Kiroro is a professional model and one of Lion Yuu's creative collaborators.
As a tutor, Kiroro brings a working model's perspective to the course and
helps students understand how direction, communication and trust shape the
experience in front of the camera.

Kiroro supports students as they practise giving clearer direction and making
photographs that feel considered from the subject's point of view.
