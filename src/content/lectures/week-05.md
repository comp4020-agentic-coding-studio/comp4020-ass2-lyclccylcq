Closes Act I by shifting from diagnosis to preparation. Before a camera is
ever raised, there is a person with a preferred side, things they dislike in
photos of themselves, a preference for natural over posed (or the reverse),
expectations shaped by what they post, reference photos they already like,
and an intended use and context for the finished image.

Introduces a reusable idea running through the rest of the course: the
**Partner Photography Profile** — a short, personal brief on how someone
wants to be photographed, gathered once and drawn on every session after.

## Themes for development

- preferred side, framing choices, known dislikes
- natural versus posed, and how to find out which someone actually prefers
- social-media expectations as an input, not an afterthought
- reference photos, intended use, and shooting context
- the Partner Photography Profile concept (format still to be designed)
