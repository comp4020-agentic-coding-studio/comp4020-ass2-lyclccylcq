Act III opens by returning to the photographer/subject relationship from Act
I, now after a shoot has happened: how photographer selection differs from
subject selection, reading micro-expressions across a near-identical burst,
the social-media context a photo will be posted into, cropping, basic
editing, skin tone, and where retouching has boundaries — including the point
at which flattering becomes falsifying.

Central question for the week: **who gets to decide which portrait is
successful?**

## Themes for development

- photographer's shortlist versus subject's shortlist
- micro-expressions across a near-identical sequence
- cropping and basic editing as selection decisions, not just technical ones
- skin tone, retouching boundaries, and flattering versus falsifying
