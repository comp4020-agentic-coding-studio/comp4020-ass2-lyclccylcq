Act I's turning point. Lays the photographer's usual criteria for a good shot
— composition, light, background, timing, technical quality — directly
alongside the subject's — expression, proportion, hair, a preferred side,
whether it "feels like them" — and shows how rarely the two rankings agree on
the same frame.

Central exercise: an independent favourite-selection comparison, each side
picking their favourite from the same set without seeing the other's pick
first.

This week states the course's thesis directly: **a successful partner
portrait is negotiated between two ways of seeing — the photographer's and
the subject's.**

## Themes for development

- photographer criteria vs. subject criteria, side by side
- the independent favourite-selection exercise (design still to be finalised)
- naming the thesis explicitly, as the hinge the rest of the semester turns on
