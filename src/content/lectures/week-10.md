Closes Act II. Hard versus soft light, the direction light comes from, shade,
window light, backlight, and the particular problem of midday sun — plus
skin exposure and how a distracting environment competes with the subject.

Practical framing for the week: a beautiful location or a good sunset is not
automatically a flattering portrait — light that looks good to the eye and
light that looks good on a face are not the same claim.

## Themes for development

- hard vs. soft light, and where each comes from in an ordinary setting
- shade, window light, backlight, midday sun as recurring real situations
- skin exposure and distracting environments
- separating "beautiful location" from "flattering portrait"
