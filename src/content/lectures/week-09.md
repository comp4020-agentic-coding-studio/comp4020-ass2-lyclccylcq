Technical content framed entirely through practical portrait problems, not as
standalone optics theory: focal length and working distance, wide-angle
distortion up close, how a standard or telephoto perspective reads on a face,
the difference between a smartphone's 0.5x, 1x and 2x cameras, and the choice
between an environmental portrait and a tighter one.

## Themes for development

- focal length and working distance as a pair, not focal length alone
- wide-angle distortion at close range on a face
- smartphone 0.5x / 1x / 2x as a familiar, accessible version of this idea
- environmental portrait versus tighter portrait as a deliberate choice
