Act II opens with the first concrete technical fix, introduced only as the
answer to a complaint from Act I: camera height relative to the subject,
shooting distance, and how both change apparent proportion — legs, height,
the whole body in frame.

## Themes for development

- camera height and its effect on perceived height and leg length
- shooting distance and its effect on perspective distortion
- full-body framing choices as a deliberate decision, not a default
