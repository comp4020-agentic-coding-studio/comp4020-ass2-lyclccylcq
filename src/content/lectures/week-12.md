Closing week, and the semester's synthesis rather than new material: before a
shoot (preferences, intent, references, location, equipment), during it
(position, frame, direction, adapting, light, communication), and after it
(shortlisting, comparing, editing, delivering) — the whole arc the semester
has built toward, in one sequence.

Closing line for the course: **a technically strong image is not enough if
the person in it does not recognise themselves or want to keep it.**

## Themes for development

- before / during / after as the shoot's three phases
- drawing the phase back to specific earlier weeks (mapping still to be done)
- the closing line, and what it asks a final shoot to demonstrate
