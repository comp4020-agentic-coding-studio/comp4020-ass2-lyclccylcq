Direction technique, following on from week 7's posture work. Key idea for
the week: telling someone to "act natural" is not direction, it's an
instruction with no content — and it usually produces the opposite of what it
asks for.

Looks instead at prompts versus fixed poses, movement, conversation as a
distraction technique, anticipating a moment rather than waiting for it,
reading for genuine expression, and shooting in bursts to catch it.

## Themes for development

- why "act natural" fails as direction
- prompts and movement in place of static posing instructions
- conversation and distraction as ways to get past self-consciousness
- anticipation, genuine expression, and burst shooting
