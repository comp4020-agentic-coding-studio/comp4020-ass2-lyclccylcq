A second, distinct failure mode from week 2's: the travel photo. Landmark
squarely behind the subject, near-identical poses across a whole trip, a
tiny figure lost against a cluttered background — all symptoms of treating a
photo as proof of presence rather than as something worth looking at.

Framing question for the week: **when does a photograph become evidence
rather than a memory?**

## Themes for development

- landmark-in-front-of-subject as a default, not a choice
- repeated, interchangeable poses across a trip's worth of photos
- subject scale against a cluttered or competing background
- proof-of-presence versus a photo someone would actually choose to keep
