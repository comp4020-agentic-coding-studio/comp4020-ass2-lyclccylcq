Tutorial participation is a cumulative assessment component rather than a
fourth assignment. It runs across ten consecutive tutorials from **Week 2 to
Week 11** and contributes 20% of the final course mark.

## Marks in each tutorial

| Component | Mark per tutorial | Total across 10 tutorials |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Attendance and participation | 1 | 10 |
| Photograph submitted during the tutorial | 1 | 10 |
| **Total** | **2** | **20** |

The participation mark is awarded for attending and taking part in the
tutorial activities. The photograph mark requires the tutorial photograph to
be submitted during that tutorial; the final date above is the last marked
tutorial, not a later submission window for earlier photographs.
