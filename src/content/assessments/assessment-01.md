## Brief

> Use the concepts taught in the course to critically analyse a supplied set
> of photographs and the date and shoot plan that produced them.

You will receive a set of photographs together with the corresponding plan for
the date and the shoot. Treat the photographs and the plan as connected: the
arrangements shape what was possible, while the photographs show the results
of those choices.

Your report must state which parts you like or consider successful and explain
why. Where you do not like the result, identify the specific shortcomings,
explain what caused them, and propose realistic improvements to the plan or the
photographic decisions. Personal preference is welcome, but every conclusion
must be supported by evidence from the supplied material and relevant course
theory.

## Deliverables

Submit one report that:

- evaluates the supplied photographs and the date and shoot plan;
- identifies successful and unsuccessful choices;
- applies course concepts to explain those judgements; and
- recommends specific improvements that could produce a stronger experience
  and photographic result.

This is an analysis task. You do not need to produce new photographs for this
assessment.

## Marking approach

The teaching team marks the report. There is no separate photography mark for
this task. Strong work connects theory to precise observations rather than
listing course concepts or relying only on taste.
